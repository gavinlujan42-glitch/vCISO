Accessibility Statement

The site aims to provide an inclusive experience aligned with WCAG 2.2 Level AA principles.

Statement

Design goals include keyboard navigation, visible focus, semantic structure, readable contrast, responsive layouts, text alternatives and reduced-motion support where practical. Accessibility is an ongoing engineering responsibility, not a one-time claim of perfect conformance. If a page or feature creates a barrier, contact Gavin Lujan through the public contact method on the main site and identify the page, problem and preferred format.
