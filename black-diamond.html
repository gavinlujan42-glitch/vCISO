vCISO // BLACK DIAMOND

Incident response with an executive objective.

Black Diamond extends vCISO governance into authorized incident response and digital forensics. The operating model connects technical evidence to executive decisions, recovery priorities, risk ownership and post-incident control improvement.

OBJECTIVE-BASED ENGAGEMENT

OBJECTIVE → SCOPE → CONTAIN → INVESTIGATE → EVIDENCE → FINDINGS → HARDEN

Each engagement defines authority, scope, assumptions, evidence requirements, deliverables and completion criteria before execution.

Capabilities include incident command, compromise assessment, digital forensics, evidence preservation, recovery validation and executive findings. Legal and regulatory evidence requirements should be coordinated with qualified counsel and appropriate authorities.
